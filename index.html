    How this works

    1. Pick your name below, then add your time zone — it saves automatically.
    2. Paint your real availability further down, on actual dates, in your own local time. Browse forward with the arrows to mark out several weeks.
    3. Compare against anyone on your list — their free hours convert into your time zone, and overlapping slots show up as "Times you're both free" with a one-click Book button. No outside scheduling tool needed.
    4. Booking sets the pair to Booked with the exact time. Mark it Done once the call happens — either person can update a pair, since it's shared.
